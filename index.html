From glTF to Cycles

The self-authored geometry is exported as binary glTF 2.0, then imported into an empty scene. Full material IDs select the Cycles finishes; unknown IDs are rejected before any replacement. Geometry, UVs, normals and portable textures come from glTF. Cycles-specific shader nodes are rebuilt through the ID mapping.

All eleven images are direct Cycles renders with consistent studio lighting and exposure, without per-image manual compositing. Blender 5.1.2 · OptiX · up to 256 samples · AgX · transparent film.

This sample uses synthetic material IDs. Actual customer models and their authoritative material IDs have not been received or tested. The demonstration does not establish compatibility with an unseen customer asset set.

Woven fabric and wood are shown on the chair and separate swatches. The other four finishes are swatch studies. Bouclé uses procedural yarn-loop geometry and may appear stylized at extreme close range; a full upholstered sofa and its seams have not been demonstrated. Wood end grain is approximate. Satin glass includes studio reflections and transmission, so its appearance is not independent of the intended composite background. Named commercial finishes require their own visual references.
